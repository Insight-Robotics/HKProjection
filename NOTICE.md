## Derived data — `hk_height_model.csv`

`hk_height_model.csv` is generated by `tools/extract_control_points.py` from
*Control Points for Height Model of Hong Kong (Version 1.0)*. The extraction
script is MIT-licensed as part of this project, but **the underlying survey
data is the Lands Department's, not ours**.
